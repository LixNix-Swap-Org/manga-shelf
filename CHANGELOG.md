# Changelog

## 3.5.0

### Before updating
- **Database migrations 34 to 36** run at the first start: `animes.tags` (tags of the anime list), the table `anime_airings` (aired episodes for the calendar history) and, for webtoons, `mangas.format` (`print`, the default for every existing series, or `webtoon`), `mangas.total_chapters` and the table `chapter_progress` (each user's chapter of a webtoon). As always the backup `backups/vor-update-v<old>-auf-v<new>-…zip` is written first. Because the schema changes, 3.4.0 cannot open the migrated database.
- **Going back to 3.4.0 restores the backup from before the update, and everything changed since is lost** (new series and volumes, reading state, anime progress, shopping list, users and settings; images in `uploads/` stay). Where the server updates itself (Pterodactyl, a headless binary in a folder of your own), "Zurück zu v3.4.0" in System → Updates does this after you confirm the loss. Every other install type gets the panel with the steps in the same place; the steps are also in the README, "Updating → Going back".
- **Calendar subscriptions:** a release radar address you already subscribed to keeps listing volumes only. The new anime calendar is a subscription of its own with its own address ("Kalender abonnieren" in the tab "Kalender" of the Anime-Radar); a new or ended address of one calendar leaves the other working.

### Webtoons
- **Webtoons and manhwa with chapters:** a series can be "Webtoon (Kapitel)" instead of "Gedruckt (Bände)" (add dialog and edit form, with an optional "Kapitel gesamt"). On its page a panel "Kapitel" shows your chapter as "150 von 200 Kapiteln gelesen" with a bar, "+1" and "Gelesen bis Kapitel" (with "Rückgängig" in the notice), and how far the other people of the series are. Everyone has their own chapter; a viewer of the shelf may set theirs too, like reading marks. Offline the panel only shows. The card and the list row show "Webtoon", "150 / 200 Kap." and a chapter bar, sorting by completion uses the chapter progress, the collecting filter has "Webtoons" (once there is one), and a webtoon counts for "Mit Lücken" only with a print volume marked as missing.
- **Out of the print flows:** Manga Passion leaves webtoons alone: no gap check, no ghost spines, no autofill or edition link, no calendar matches (409 `MP_WEBTOON` for scripts), and "Serie ohne Manga-Passion-Verknüpfung" in "Sammlung aufräumen" skips them. Print volumes you add to a webtoon behave as before (shopping list, radar, value). A new webtoon gets no Manga Passion link, but a print series that becomes a webtoon in the edit form keeps its link, so turning it back into print brings gaps, calendar and autofill back; a language other than German still removes it.
- **Lookup:** an AniList hit of a Korean or Chinese comic and a MyAnimeList hit of the type Manhwa or Manhua carry the badge "Manhwa" or "Manhua". In the add dialog such a hit suggests the webtoon format and fills in the chapter count when the source knows it, and picking a Manga Passion hit sets the format back to print. The lookup of the edit form never changes the format of a stored series: a webtoon hit on a print series shows "Treffer ist ein Webtoon – Form bleibt Druck", and only a webtoon takes over the chapter count. Such a hit is no longer folded into a Manga Passion hit of the same title, so "Solo Leveling" offers the German print edition and the webtoon side by side.
- **Shelves and trash:** the chapter progress follows the rules of reading marks: the trash keeps it, leaving a shelf or a move into a shelf you are not a member of deletes it, and the questions before that name it.
- **Statistics:** the reading history shows the chapters read in webtoons, in how many series and how many are read to their end.
- **CSV and device:** the CSV has the columns "Format", "Gesamtkapitel" and "Kapitel gelesen von" ("anna: 150, ben: 30"; the import applies the reader rules: members of the target shelf, others only by an admin, new series only). The format is part of the series match, so a print edition and a webtoon of the same title stay two series and every row lands in the one of its own format: like the language, the export names the format only on the first row of a series and the rows after it inherit it, an empty "Format" cell there means print, and a file without the column is print throughout. A name in "Kapitel gelesen von" may contain a colon ("Dr: Who: 7"; the number after the last colon counts). "Vom Server holen" without admin rights takes your own chapter progress along.
- **Scripts:** `POST`/`PUT /api/mangas` take `format` (`print`, `webtoon`) and `total_chapters`; `PUT /api/mangas/:id` keeps `manga_passion_id` when only the format changes (it goes with `manga_passion_id: null` or a language other than `de`); `PUT /api/mangas/:id/chapters` sets the own chapter (`{ chapters_read }`, `{ add }`, optional `monotonic`; 400 `CHAPTER_ABOVE_TOTAL` above a known total); `GET /api/mangas` rows carry `format`, `total_chapters` and the caller's `chapters_read`, the detail also `chapter_progress`; `GET /api/stats/reading` carries `chapters`; the move preview and the shelf members carry `chapters`; lookup hits carry `series_format` and `total_chapters`.

### Anime
- **Seasons of a series:** when an anime is one season of a longer run, its detail lists the whole line as "Staffeln" (season 1, 2, 3, ...) with your status and a button to open or add each one. The line follows the TV prequel and sequel relations, first through the seasons that are already in your list (their stored relations, no request) and only beyond the last of them through AniList (`GET /api/anime/:id/season-chain`, one request per step, at most 12 per call). Every step is cached for a day, so the neighbouring seasons of the same run reuse it, and a step that fails (503) keeps the steps before it for the next try. The line also shows seasons that are not in your list yet, with a button to add them; without AniList in `ANIME_SOURCES` it follows only the entries in your list and asks nothing. Films and specials stay in their own block.
- **Films and specials:** the anime detail lists related films, specials and OVAs in their own block "Filme & Specials", apart from the other related anime (music videos stay with the related anime). They do not change the episode count. Each film shows your own status, and a one-episode film no longer shows the single-episode grid.
- **Seasons set by hand:** the sections of the episode grid now count their episodes from 1 and name the real episode numbers in their heading; 3.4.0 numbered the cells from the first episode of the anime.
- **Seasons without a click:** a long anime (more than 100 episodes; a running show without a known count by its aired episodes) that has no seasons set by hand asks TMDB for the show's seasons when the detail dialog opens and shows them in the episode grid in place of the blocks of 100, named like "Staffel 4 · Pain-Invasion · Folgen 97–156" (the real episode numbers) with the cells of every season counting from 1 (for example the arcs of One Piece). Nothing is stored until someone presses "Für alle übernehmen" next to the grid (or "Staffeln bearbeiten" saves seasons by hand), then they apply for everybody. Without a TMDB key, a match or an answer the grid keeps its blocks. The server keeps the answer for 14 days (a miss for one day). Title matching against TMDB now ignores macrons and other accents on Latin letters ("Naruto: Shippuden" = "Naruto Shippūden"), so such titles find their seasons too.
- **Anime search:** the kind chips of "Anime hinzufügen" start on "Anime", so a search finds only anime (AniList and MyAnimeList) until you pick "Alle", "Serien" or "Filme". A choice you made earlier on the device is still remembered.
- **Anime-Radar tabs:** besides "Neue Folgen" and "Manga-Verknüpfung" the Anime-Radar has the tabs "Kalender", "Gemeinsam" and "Entdecken" (below); on a phone the tabs wrap into a second row instead of scrolling sideways.
- **Calendar:** the tab "Kalender" of the Anime-Radar shows the airing times of the anime you watch or plan (Schaue, Geplant) by week, month or year (the choice is remembered on the device, "Nur meine Anime" switches to all entries). A running TV show continues weekly for three more weeks as estimates ("geschätzt", dashed). The past shows the episodes that aired since this version recorded them (a check mark for the ones you watched) and the days you finished an entry ("Abgeschlossen"). "Kalender abonnieren" gives an iCal address with the same times (timed events, a reminder can be set in the calendar app, which also works on Android with the app closed). It is an address of its own: it lists only the anime, the release radar's address only volumes, and a new or ended address of one leaves the other working; "Alle Sitzungen beenden", a password change or reset and deleting the user end both. Scripts: `GET`/`POST`/`DELETE /api/anime/feed-token` (the same answers as `/api/radar/feed-token`), `GET /api/anime/feed.ics?token=` (only with that token; a radar token answers 404), `GET /api/anime/history?from=&to=` (aired episodes and finished days).
- **Together:** the tab "Gemeinsam" lists entries that two or more accounts follow: who is how far, "Gemeinsam bis Folge N" and a spoiler warning for the people in front. It appears once another account follows one of the entries, so a server with one account does not show it.
- **Discover:** the tab "Entdecken" has "Für dich" (AniList recommendations after the anime you rated 8 or higher or finished without a rating, entries already in the list left out, "Weil du … mochtest") and "Season" (a season of AniList by popularity, "Mehr anzeigen"), each with "Zur Liste"; the score is written in the number format of the interface language. Scripts: `GET /api/anime/discover/season` and `GET /api/anime/discover/recommendations` (cached for 6 hours, they ask AniList with your own key first, then the shared pool); without AniList in `ANIME_SOURCES` both answer 503 `SOURCES_UNAVAILABLE` and ask nothing.
- **Tags:** every entry can carry tags (at most 20 of 30 characters, shared by everybody) in the detail dialog; the anime tab filters by tag. Adding or removing a tag changes only that tag, so two people tagging the same entry at once both keep theirs. Without edit rights the tag block of the detail appears only when the entry has tags. Scripts: `PUT /api/anime/:id` takes `tags` (`null` clears) or `tags_add` / `tags_remove`, entries carry `tags`.
- **Spoiler guard:** a switch in the detail dialog (per device, off by default) hides the description until you watched the first episode and the ratings of others until you finished the entry or rated it yourself ("Trotzdem anzeigen" shows them once).
- **Episode alerts:** the switch "Hinweis, wenn eine neue Folge erscheint" in "Neue Folgen" announces each aired episode of your anime once while Manga Shelf is open, as a notice and, when the browser allows it, as a system notification.
- **Statistics:** the card "Mein Anime-Jahr" shows the year's finished entries, episodes, watch time and average rating, finished entries per month, the spread of your ratings and your genres and studios (`anime.insights` of `GET /api/stats`).
- **Linked to a series in another shelf:** an anime linked to a series in a shelf you cannot see is no longer listed under "Ohne Verknüpfung" with a suggestion, and its series select in the detail is disabled (a new link for it answers 404), so nobody overwrites a link they cannot see. Scripts: the list and the detail carry `manga_linked_elsewhere: true` for it, never the id or title of that series.
- **Sharing on a server with one account:** the visibility select, the comment and the community block stay hidden even when your default visibility is "Alle anonym" or "Nur ich"; only a comment you already wrote keeps them.
- **Pause after a 429:** an anime source (AniList, Jikan, MyAnimeList, TMDb) is paused for at most 5 minutes after a 429, whatever `Retry-After` or the reset header says; before, a huge value could block it for hours.

### Shopping list
- **Shop scan as a basket:** the scan list now also books volumes that are not on the shopping list. A stored volume that is ordered, announced or missing in a paused series is booked as your purchase, and a volume of a series you already collect that is not stored yet is created as owned with today's purchase date ("wird beim Buchen angelegt"; this needs a connection). "N als gekauft abhaken" books all of them in one go, the list shows the price total per currency, and every scan can be removed. A series that is not in the collection yet is still only noted, and a volume of a series in a shelf where you are a viewer stays unbooked with "… In diesem Regal bist du Leser und kannst hier nicht buchen". When the server refuses one volume because of its shelf, the basket still books the others; a read-only account or an ended session stops it.

### Manga Passion
- **Double volumes:** an edition that publishes two volumes as one book ("10 & 11", Eyeshield 21 at Egmont) now lists every volume on its own. The gap check, the gap import and the shelf see volumes 10 and 11 (the price of the book is split between them), so the series counts 1 to 37 without a hole; before, "10 & 11" was stored as one entry without a number and sorted behind the last volume. The edition's total counts volumes instead of books, so "Bandzahl anpassen" no longer offers 19 for 37 volumes. A "10 & 11" entry imported earlier stays: delete it and import the gaps again. Schuber and special editions with a number range stay one entry.
- **Schuber dates:** the date autofill gives "Schuber N" the N-th box set by release date again: a box set without a date comes last (before, it took the place of Schuber 1 and shifted every other date by one), a month-only date after the exact days of its month, and the same day keeps Manga Passion's order. Sorting the volumes by "Erscheinungsjahr" now uses the whole release date (day, month or year as known, undated entries last) and the number for a tie.

### Apps
- **Release-day reminders (Android; iPhone should work but is untested):** the switch "Erinnerungen am Erscheinungstag" (account dialog, tab "Sprache"; without a server also on the "Server" screen) is off by default, per device and for editors and admins. Switching it on asks for the notification permission; switching it off removes every reminder, also the ones already shown. The app plans local notifications on the device from the release radar (volumes with an exact release day) and the anime list (the next episode of your "Schaue" and "Geplant" entries, when its airing is confirmed): one notification per day for the next 14 days at 9:00 local time, several volumes and episodes of a day together. It plans again at every start, return to the foreground and move to the background (at most every 15 minutes), and at once after a change in the app that can move a reminder (a volume bought, owned, added, edited or deleted, its owners, a taken-over release date, a purchase from the shopping list or the shop basket). Every release day keeps its notification, so planning again never removes a reminder that is already showing. The planned reminders stay while offline. Signing out, the end of the session, another account, another server or another profile of the device removes the pending and the shown reminders and turns the switch off, so the next account starts without them; restoring a backup in the app keeps the switch and plans again. The hint "Mitteilungen sind für Manga Shelf ausgeschaltet – …" also appears when the permission was taken back in the system settings. No server change and no push service; it works without a server too. Android: its own notification channel (lock-screen visibility private), a monochrome book icon in the status bar, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` for Android 13+, no exact-alarm permission; after a phone restart the plugin restores the scheduled reminders. Native: `@capacitor/local-notifications` 7, bridge version 4 (`plugins.LocalNotifications`). `Podfile.lock` gets the new pod at the next `pod install` on macOS.
- **Signed Android app:** the release APK and AAB are signed with the project's release key from now on, so later versions install over this one. An APK of an earlier release was signed with a throwaway key: uninstall it once before installing this one. The signature check of the build also reads the `apksigner` output of Android build-tools 37 ("V3.0 Signer: certificate …").

### Pterodactyl and server
- **Egg as a release file, automatic install:** `egg-manga-shelf.json` is a file of its own in every release (listed in `SHA256SUMS.txt`). Its installation downloads the latest `pterodactyl-manga-shelf.zip` from the releases and unpacks it into the server folder, so a new server starts without uploading the ZIP; a failed download ends the installation with the hint to upload the ZIP by hand. The egg offers the images Node.js 22 (the default), 24 and 26, the switch "Behind a reverse proxy" (it adds the gateway of the Docker network to `TRUST_PROXY`) and the variables `APP_TIMEZONE`, `BACKUP_HOUR`, `BACKUP_KEEP_DAILY`, `LOG_LEVEL`, `UPDATE_INSTALL` and `SETUP_TOKEN`; the variable "Server Port" is gone (Pterodactyl sets `SERVER_PORT` from the allocation), and the stop button sends Ctrl+C (the old `^^C` is no signal Wings knows, so it killed the server). Import the egg again to get all of this.
- **A new database** logs one line ("Neue Datenbank angelegt (Schema v36, 36 Migrationen, … ms)") instead of one line per migration.

### PWA
- **Cover cache:** the service worker keeps at most 1000 cover images in its uploads cache and drops the oldest first, so the cache no longer grows without limit on phones with large collections.

- **Request parsing:** `body-parser` 2.4.0 and `ip-address` 10.7.4 (GHSA-27r3-jxmh-p828).

## 3.4.0

### Before updating
- **Database migrations 29 to 33** run at the first start: `anime_progress.watched_episodes` (single watched episodes), `anime_progress.episode_scores` (ratings of single episodes) and `animes.custom_seasons` (seasons set by hand), shelves (`shelves`, `shelf_members`, `mangas.shelf_id`, `trash.shelf_id`) and the visibility of anime ratings (`anime_progress.rating_visibility`, `public_comment`, `users.default_anime_visibility`). As always the backup `backups/vor-update-v<old>-auf-v<new>-…zip` is written first. Because the schema changes, 3.3.0 cannot open the migrated database.
- **After the update everybody shares one shelf:** every series and every trash entry lands in the household shelf "Sammlung", and every account becomes a member of it (admins as owners, editors as editors, guests as viewers), so nothing changes until someone creates another shelf. Existing anime ratings are public, as everybody could see them before. From now on a server admin sees only the shelves they are a member of; a backup still holds every shelf.
- **Going back to 3.3.0 restores the backup from before the update, and everything changed since is lost** (new series and volumes, reading state, anime progress, shopping list, users and settings; images in `uploads/` stay). Where the server updates itself (Pterodactyl, a headless binary in a folder of your own), "Zurück zu v3.3.0" in System → Updates does this after you confirm the loss. Every other install type gets the panel with the steps in the same place, because 3.3.0 already records the version before a change; the steps are also in the README, "Updating → Going back".

### Shelves
- **Shelves:** every series belongs to one shelf, and an account sees only the shelves it is a member of, as owner, editor or viewer. The shelf, search, statistics, shopping list, radar, calendar feed, trash, offline copy and CSV export cover the caller's shelves only; a series, volume or shelf elsewhere answers 404, so its id cannot be probed. With one shelf nothing changes on screen: the shelf filter and the shelf choice when adding or editing a series appear only once there is more than one.
- **New accounts join the household shelf** ("Haushaltsregal", the default in the user dialog). The dialog offers three alternatives, each with a line on what it does: "Eigenes Regal" (an empty shelf of its own, "Sammlung von <name>", that only the new account sees), "Regal eines Benutzers mitnutzen" (membership in the shelf of the chosen user) and "Gegenseitig mit dir teilen" (a shelf of its own plus access to yours, and you get access to the new one). Joining or sharing a shelf needs you to own it, the granted role never exceeds the role of the user it is shared from, a guest always joins as viewer and gets no shelf of its own (the dialog does not offer "Eigenes Regal" and "Gegenseitig mit dir teilen" for it), and a new admin account joins as editor, never as owner. Scripts: `POST /api/users` takes `shelf_mode` (`household`, the default, `personal`, `share_same`, `share_mutual`) with `share_with_user_id` (for `share_mutual` only your own id) and `share_role` (`editor`, `viewer`); `separate_shelf: true` still means `personal`. A refused choice answers 400 and creates no account.
- **What each role may do:** owners rename a shelf, change its colour, pick members from the list of accounts, change their roles and remove them; editors add, change and delete series and volumes; viewers see everything in the shelf and mark their own reading state (also "Bis Band X als gelesen"), but toggle no ownership, edit nothing and get no delete button on the cards. Guest accounts are never more than viewers (400 `SHELF_ROLE_CAPPED`). Every editor or admin can create a shelf ("Werkzeuge" → "Regale") and becomes its owner.
- **A shelf keeps an owner:** only owners whose account is admin or editor count, and the last of them can neither leave nor give up the role (409 `SHELF_LAST_OWNER`; the dialog then shows them a hint instead of the role select and "Verlassen"). A change of the account role follows in the shelves: an admin who becomes editor stays owner only where nobody else can manage the shelf, a guest who becomes editor or admin turns from viewer into editor in the household shelf, and an editor or admin who becomes guest is a viewer everywhere; when that would leave a shelf without an owner, the change is refused with 409 and the message names the shelf.
- **Admins see no foreign shelves:** a server admin sees only the shelves they are a member of, and the user management does not reach into the others: a new account joins or shares only a shelf the admin owns, ownership and reading marks for someone else only go into shelves that person is a member of, and an admin takes over a shelf of a deleted account only when no other member can manage it. A backup is the exception: it holds the whole database.
- **Moving a series:** only an owner of its shelf can move it ("Regal" in the edit form, shown when you own the shelf and can write to another one), and only into a shelf where they are at least editor. The ownership and reading marks of everybody who is not a member of the target shelf are deleted: before the move the app names each of them with their owned and read volumes and asks, and moving the series back does not bring the marks back. Deleted volumes of the series in the trash follow it. Scripts: `GET /api/mangas/:id/shelf-move?shelf_id=` answers who would lose marks (`affected: [{ user_id, username, owned, read }]`), `PUT /api/mangas/:id/shelf` moves.
- **Removing a member and leaving:** that member's ownership and reading marks on the shelf's volumes go with the membership; the question before it names how many there are and says that they are deleted. A volume that loses its last owner becomes "Fehlt". Scripts: the members in `GET /api/shelves/:id` carry `marks: { owned, read }`.
- **Deleting a shelf:** only an empty one, never the household shelf; what is still in its trash goes with it, and the question says so.
- **Shelf filter:** with more than one shelf the collection toolbar offers a shelf filter, remembered per server and account; "Zurücksetzen" in the toolbar and in an empty result clears it. While it is set, statistics (reading history included), trash, shopping list and "Meine Vorbestellungen & Budget" follow it and show "Regal: <name>" with a button that shows every shelf again; the Manga Passion calendar is the same for everybody. A shopping list narrowed to one shelf is never kept as the offline copy. The shelf names load again after a change in the shelves dialog and when a shelf is gone.
- **Trash:** the trash lists what was deleted in your shelves, and emptying it (admins) removes the entries of the shelves you own or are an editor of. A restored series goes back into the shelf it was deleted from, else into the shelf of its trash entry, each only while that shelf exists and you may edit there (otherwise 409 `SHELF_UNAVAILABLE`, never into a shelf of your own instead); a restored volume goes back into the shelf its series is in now, which needs editor rights there. Ownership and reading marks come back only for the current members of that shelf.
- **People in a shelf:** the readers and owners shown for a series, the per-person figures of the statistics, the owner and reader names of a CSV import (others get the warning "… gehört nicht zu diesem Regal (ignoriert)") and the marks an admin enters for someone else only reach members of the shelf; the statistics also name whoever already holds a mark there. Someone else's reading statistics (`GET /api/users/:id/stats`, `GET /api/stats/reading?user_id=`) answer 404 unless you share a shelf or are admin.
- **Deleting an account:** volumes only that account owned go to another member of their shelf (an owner first; the deleting admin only when a member there), otherwise they become "Fehlt". A shelf it was the last owner of goes to another member who can manage it, else to the deleting admin; an empty personal shelf nobody else uses is deleted.
- **Checks per shelf:** an id like `1x` or `+1` in a series or volume path answers 404 instead of skipping the shelf check, and a `manga_id` like `1e1` or `0x1` for new volumes is refused (400); the gap check stores an edition link only for editors and owners of the series' shelf, and a viewer's volume lookup stores no edition link and downloads no cover; the fixes of "Sammlung aufräumen" change only shelves you may edit, and a "Gelesen" volume without an owner goes to you there. The anime tab names no series of a shelf you cannot see (`manga_id` and `manga` are `null`, `GET /api/mangas/:id/adaptations` answers 404).
- **Tools:** "Werkzeuge" in the header gathers "Regale", "Sammlung aufräumen", "Papierkorb" and, for admins, "Verlage zusammenführen", the phone menu likewise ("Werkzeuge & Regale"); the statistics footer keeps its links. Below the 2xl width the button shows only its icon (the name stays its label) and the search box may shrink to 150 px, so the desktop header with the install button fits at 1280 px and "Abmelden" stays visible. Escape closes the list without reaching the search; offline the button is disabled, and "Regale" is missing in the mode without a server.
- **Device:** "Vom Server holen" without admin rights (the offline copy) puts every series into the device's one shelf, whichever shelf it came from on the server.
- **Scripts:** `GET /api/mangas` rows carry `shelf_id`; `POST /api/mangas`, `POST /api/import/csv` and `POST /api/manga-passion/import` take `shelf_id` (default: a shelf you created and may write to, else the household shelf); everything that lists or sums series (`/mangas`, `/mangas/volume-search`, `/tags`, `/offline-snapshot`, `/stats`, `/stats/reading`, `/shopping-list`, `/release-radar`, `/dashboard-summary`, `/trash`, `/export/csv`) takes `?shelf_id=` to narrow it to one shelf (a foreign one answers 404). The shelves and their members are under `/api/shelves`, the accounts to pick from under `/api/users/directory`.

### Dialogs
- **Confirmation dialog:** deleting a series, one or several volumes, an account or a server snapshot, filling volumes from Manga Passion, removing a server, switching the device to a server and dropping unsaved edits ask in the app's own dialog instead of the browser's `confirm()`, which embedded browsers suppress. It covers the whole screen (also on the series page and inside the anime dialog), focuses "Abbrechen" first and shows one question at a time. Back, the Android back button and leaving the page answer it with no, and while it is open the shortcuts of the series page (J, K, E, Space) do nothing.
- **Unsaved edits:** closing the anime dialog, "Öffnen" on a related anime and the "Reihe" link ask before unsaved progress, a rating or a comment is dropped; Escape in the edit form of a series asks the same.

### Collection
- **"Komplett" and progress:** a series is only complete, and shows 100 %, when you own every regular volume up to the highest one that exists in it. With "Gesamtbände: 3" and a missing volume 4 the card now reads "3 / 4" (75 %) instead of "3 / 3", the series counts for "Mit Lücken" and not for "Komplett". Scripts: `max_regular_number` in `GET /api/mangas` is now the highest regular number of any status (before: owned only), and `completed_series` in `GET /api/stats` follows the same target.

### Scan
- **ISBN lookup:** the Open Library cover URLs that `GET /api/lookup/isbn` answers (DNB and K10plus hits, and Google Books hits without a thumbnail of their own) end in `?default=false`, so an ISBN Open Library has no cover for gives a failed image request instead of a 1×1 placeholder.

- **Passwords:** restoring a backup (the Backups dialog, the app's transfer to a server, and scripts using `/api/backups/:filename/restore`, `/api/backup/restore`, `/api/restore` or `/api/backup/restore/:stagingId`) now needs your current password, like installing an update. "Auf vX aktualisieren", or another version plus "Herunterladen und prüfen", downloads and checks in the background (the dialog may be closed, "Abbrechen" discards the download); "Installieren" asks for your password. The page waits for the restart, reloads by itself and says whether it worked or rolled back; after 3 minutes it shows where to look (panel console, journalctl, launchd log or the start command).
- **Everywhere else the exact command or link** for the chosen version: Docker (`docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`, or the `image:` line of another version), `.deb`/`.rpm`, the Linux system service, the Windows task, the desktop app, source installs.
- **Admin notes:** a `### Before updating` block of a CHANGELOG section is copied into the release text; the confirm step shows the blocks of the target and of every release in between.
- **Checked before anything is written:** HTTPS to GitHub's hosts only, a Sigstore signature of `SHA256SUMS.txt` by the release workflow on `main` of this repository (identity, issuer and the certificate's repository, owner, trigger, runner and branch pinned), the signed version marker, the SHA-256 of the file, then the new ZIP or binary itself (version, Node.js range, syntax, dependencies, a test run and a migration check on a copy). A check stays valid for 15 minutes.
- **No data loss:** a verified backup first, one update at a time (restoring, creating and deleting backups, orphan cleanup and the daily snapshot wait), writes refused from the backup to the restart, a journaled switch that the next start completes or undoes, and an automatic rollback of the code and, if the database moved on, of the database (Pterodactyl after the first failed start, elsewhere after the second; a database the new version had already written is kept as `manga.db.v<version>-<time>`, and a rollback that cannot finish stops the server with exit code 74 and is resumed at the next start).
- **Restart:** exit code 75, which Wings, systemd (`Restart=on-failure`) and launchd (`KeepAlive`) answer with a start. A binary started by hand (also on Windows) exits cleanly after the switch and the page shows the command to start it again.

### Release pipeline
- **Signed release files:** the new job `sign` (environment `release` with required reviewers; the only job with `id-token: write`, it also signs the Docker image, which the `docker` job no longer does) writes the version marker `manga-shelf-release-vX.Y.Z.json`, `SHA256SUMS.txt` (now with the marker) and its keyless cosign signature `SHA256SUMS.txt.sigstore.json`; `scripts/release/verify-bundle.js` checks the fresh bundle with the server's own verifier before anything is published, and `publish` (no `id-token`) only verifies the files again and uploads them. The job refuses a private repository. A release only starts when `main` is protected and the environment `release` has required reviewers (README, "Updating").
- **Release text:** names the in-app update, the `cosign verify-blob` command with the signer identity, and starts with the `### Before updating` block of the version's CHANGELOG section.
- **Pterodactyl ZIP** carries `.manga-shelf-release.json` (format 1, version, file list); `package.js` refuses a `package.json` without a valid `X.Y.Z` version and a list that differs from the ZIP content.
- **Recovery:** if `sign` fails after the tag exists, use "Re-run failed jobs"; nothing was published and `latest` did not move.

### Security
- **Passwords:** restoring a backup, changing your own account and creating or promoting an admin in the user management ask for your current password (same lock as sign-in); wrong entries count toward it. Applying an update, creating users, promotions and password resets log the admin, the auth scheme (cookie or bearer) and the client address.
- **Offline restore and database check:** `manga-shelf-server restore <backup.zip> [--allow-newer-schema]` and `node scripts/admin.js wiederherstellen <backup.zip>` restore a backup into a stopped server with the checks of the Backups dialog; `db-check <file>` (both) tests on a copy whether this version can open and migrate a database. A restore confirmed with "allow newer schema" is remembered, so the server starts afterwards.
- `install-service` marks new systemd units and LaunchAgents as supervised (`MANGA_SHELF_SUPERVISOR`).

### Apps

### Apps and distribution
- **Domain core `core/`:** every domain endpoint as a pure handler with a route table; the server mounts them, the apps call them directly on the device (same answers, parity tests).
- **App without a server:** collection on the device (sql.js), profiles, backup as a ZIP in the server format, takeover onto a server or from the server; failure-safe saving and protection when the collection is open in two windows.
- **Desktop app (Electron):** only on this device, as a client of a server or as a server for the home network (tray, autostart, QR code to connect).
- **Android/iPhone (Capacitor):** server list with tokens in secure storage, deep links, downloads through the share dialog, native scanner.
- **Headless server as a binary** for Linux, Windows and macOS with service installation, plus `.deb`/`.rpm`.
- **Server binary on Windows as LOCAL SERVICE** (another account with `--account LocalService|NetworkService|LocalSystem|<SID>|DOMÄNE\name`, also `.\name` and umlauts); the data folder gets a fresh protected ACL only for this account, SYSTEM and the Administrators; a `C:\ProgramData\manga-shelf` created by someone else, links/reparse points or foreign entries are rejected (allow-list check before the first start).
- **`.env` in the data folder** is rejected on every platform when it is a link, belongs to another user or can be changed by others (Linux/macOS `chmod 600`, Windows `icacls`); if the check cannot run, the server does not start.
- **Desktop app:** a refused switch of the operating mode (running restore or backup) is taken back and the menu is set right again; "Backup jetzt" counts as a running job; a notice when the keychain is locked and the window switches to the app view.
- **Server binary on Linux:** data folder 0750 (user service and macOS 0700), `UMask=0027` in the systemd units, log file 0600.
- **Build and release pipeline:** artifacts for every pull request and push to `main` (ZIP + SBOM, binaries, packages, Docker, desktop, mobile smoke as debug APK and simulator app), publishing only by button with a tag, multi-arch image on GHCR, checksums and signing from secrets (otherwise unsigned).
- **Releases without half states:** the Docker image first only gets the fixed tags `vX.Y.Z`/`X.Y.Z`; `X.Y` and `latest` only point at it after the GitHub release, a failed run never moves `latest`.
- **Android release signing** on the Ubuntu runners through a Gradle `signingConfig` from the secrets, checked with `apksigner`.
- **Desktop:** when the system keychain is not available, the secure store stays locked and the encrypted file unchanged (never plain text); switches of operating mode or port run one after another and are refused (with a notice) while a backup or restore runs.
- **Phone app:** the collection is saved failure-safe via `.new`/`.old` (at startup the last complete copy wins); images come directly from device storage on demand instead of all being loaded at startup.

### Security
- Sessions with a version, revocation on logout, bearer tokens for apps, session end after a restore and a password reset; "Alle Sitzungen beenden" (end all sessions).
- Rate limits per address and per account (brute-force protection without locking out the admin), per-account limits for external services.
- Cross-origin protection for writing requests, CORS only for registered origins and app origins, additional security headers (CSP, Permissions-Policy, COOP, HSTS with HTTPS).
- Uploads: magic byte check, EXIF/GPS stripping, random file names, a CSP of their own for `/uploads`; SSRF-safe image download.
- The server binary does not start on top of a `.env` in the data folder that other users may change.
- Apps: a new token of the same session (password change, "Alle Sitzungen beenden", own account in the user management) keeps the approved server addresses.
- CodeQL, Dependabot, audit gate and minimal permissions in the CI.
- A general request limit per client sits in front of every route (new runtime dependency `express-rate-limit`; `RATE_LIMIT_UMBRELLA`, default 1200 API, 3000 web app and 24000 cover requests per minute, `0` = off); above it 429 `TOO_MANY_REQUESTS` with `Retry-After`. Its counters keep the bounded keys and entry cap of the other limiters.
- Precompressed assets are only served from inside the assets folder.
- Uploads and backup restores resolve every stored file path inside its data directory (`uploads/`, `data/temp`, `DATA_DIR`) before reading, moving or deleting it; anything outside is refused with a 400.
- The service worker ignores `SKIP_WAITING`/`WARM_LANGUAGE` messages from other origins.
- Open Library covers get `?default=false` only when the host is `openlibrary.org` or a subdomain of it.
- Server ids created without `crypto.randomUUID` (plain-HTTP LAN address) and outbox entry ids use WebCrypto random bytes instead of `Math.random`.
- Docker health check: validates the server certificate (own cert file + public roots). The name comes from the certificate, and certificates without a usable host name (IP or free text as CN) are pinned to the file's certificate.
- `install-service` (Windows): a data folder with a space and a trailing backslash no longer breaks the task arguments.
- Anime descriptions: entities such as `&amp;lt;` are no longer decoded twice.
- `build:server`: the downloaded Node archive is checked against `SHASUMS256.txt` and unpacked in memory; only the extracted binary stays in `.node-cache`.
- Dependency overrides `postcss-selector-parser` ^7.1.6 (`frontend/package.json`) and `global-agent` ^4.1.3 (`desktop/package.json`) remove advisories without changing behaviour.
- The CodeQL findings of the public repository are resolved: path checks for uploads and backups, certificate validation in the Docker health check, origin check in the service worker, anchored URL checks, tag stripping that cannot be re-assembled.
